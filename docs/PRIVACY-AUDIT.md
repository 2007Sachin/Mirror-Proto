## Claims that were not literally true, and what changed

1. "The session stays with you." (landing): data lives on the server and goes to the providers. Now "Raw audio isn't stored by MIRROR. Your notes are visible only to you." (`src/pages/Landing.tsx`).
2. "Audio is not stored." (interview footer) did not say whose audio or that the provider hears it live. Now "MIRROR doesn't keep your audio." (`src/pages/Interview.tsx`), with the full hand-off on the privacy page.
3. "No account is connected." (Settings): removed.
4. Reset prototype description over-promised (row 14): reworded.
5. "Answers are never scored" would have been false for practice problems: the page limits it to interview answers and says problems are marked correct or not.
6. "Delete anytime" omits the sign-in account and provider backups: both said on the page and in the confirm panel.
