# Product Manager Interview Prep (Indian Startups)

## Overview
A concise guide to the interview rounds, key skills, and practice questions for product manager roles at Indian startups.

## Interview Rounds & Core Skills
1. **Product Sense** – market analysis, user empathy, prioritization (RICE, MoSCoW), ROI thinking.
2. **Case Studies / Problem Solving** – structured thinking, data‑driven hypotheses, metric selection.
3. **Behavioral / Fit** – leadership, conflict resolution, teamwork, storytelling (STAR).
4. **Technical / Analytical** – metrics (DAU/MAU, churn, LTV), basic SQL, A/B test design.
5. **Leadership / Execution** – roadmapping, stakeholder alignment, agile ceremonies, decision‑making.

## Sample Questions (10)

**Product Sense**
1. How would you improve the onboarding flow for a new user of a SaaS product targeting Indian SMBs?
2. Prioritize three features for a new e‑commerce platform with a limited budget, considering competitive pressure.

**Case Studies**
3. Design a feature to increase the free‑trial‑to‑paid conversion rate by 15% for a SaaS startup.
4. Estimate the total addressable market for an AI‑powered productivity tool in India and outline a go‑to‑market strategy.

**Behavioral**
5. Describe a situation where you disagreed with a senior stakeholder and how you resolved it.
6. Tell me about a project where you had to pivot the product direction based on user feedback.

**Technical**
7. Write a SQL query to list the top 5 users by monthly recurring revenue for the last quarter.
8. Identify three key metrics to measure the success of a new feature release and explain why.

**Leadership**
9. How do you align engineering, design, and sales teams around a common product vision?
10. Explain your approach to managing a product backlog with competing priorities from multiple stakeholders.

## Tips
- Start each answer with the problem, then your thought process, then the solution/answer.
- Use data or user research to back up recommendations.
- Keep answers concise (≈2‑3 minutes) and focused on impact.
