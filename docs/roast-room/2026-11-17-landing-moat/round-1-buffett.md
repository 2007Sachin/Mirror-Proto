WHY:
1. Moats, not stories. "A moat that must be continuously rebuilt will eventually be no moat at all." (S080) HiringAnt can ship "no scores" in a sprint. This is my translation to a consumer product; today it is a hypothesis, not proven.
2. Ruin is a broken promise. "We can afford to lose money but we can't afford to lose reputation." (S028) MIRROR has no revenue to lose, so its only real asset is trust. The audit shows "delete everything" is PARTLY true (sign-in account stays, provider backups) and "nothing shared" must say "no sharing feature". One overclaim on a privacy-led page is the front-page story. That is why ruin is "possible", not "none".
3. Capital efficiency. One founder, a few days. Every day on a page is a day not spent on the product that makes the page true. Option B spends the most days on the least durable thing, a copy of a rival's strengths. I would kill it. D builds a live no-signup demo, which means abuse and speech-provider cost with no revenue; that is a cash question I cannot size from this brief.
